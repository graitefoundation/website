Global Chapters & International Network

Connecting countries, institutions, researchers, and innovators through a unified global platform for responsible AI research and innovation.

A Global Research Network

One Global Mission. Multiple Country Chapters. Shared Responsibility.

GRAITE envisions a globally connected network of Country Chapters that bring together researchers, scientists, engineers, academic institutions, technology organisations, policymakers, and social innovators across nations.

Each Country Chapter will serve as a national platform for advancing AI research, scientific collaboration, education, responsible technology development, and public-interest innovation. Together, these chapters will form an interconnected international research community working towards GRAITE's shared mission of advancing safe, responsible, and transformative AI for humanity.

The global network will combine a common institutional identity and governance framework with locally responsive leadership, enabling each country to address its distinctive research priorities, technological opportunities, regulatory environments, and societal challenges.

Country Chapters

Building National Centres of Research & Collaboration

GRAITE Country Chapters will represent the organisation at the national level and act as focal points for research, education, partnerships, innovation, and responsible AI initiatives within their respective countries.

Each Country Chapter will seek to develop a national ecosystem connecting universities, research laboratories, scientific communities, industry, government institutions, civil society, and emerging innovators.

Country Chapters may undertake activities such as:

AI Research & Scientific Collaboration
National AI Safety & Responsible Technology Initiatives
Research Fellowships, Scholarships & Grants
Academic & Industry Partnerships
AI Education, Literacy & Capacity Building
National Conferences, Workshops & Research Symposia
AI Innovation Labs & Demonstration Programmes
Public-Interest Technology & Social Innovation
Research Publications & Knowledge Exchange
Engagement with National AI Policy & Governance Initiatives

Each chapter will align its activities with GRAITE's global mission while identifying opportunities that respond to the specific needs and priorities of its country.

Country Chapter Governance

Local Leadership Within a Unified Global Framework

Each Country Chapter will have its own governance structure to provide national leadership, strategic direction, operational oversight, and accountability. The structure will be designed to reflect local institutional requirements while remaining consistent with GRAITE's global governance principles, policies, and standards.

Country Chapter Governance Board

Each Country Chapter will be governed by a Country Chapter Governance Board responsible for guiding and overseeing the chapter's national activities. The Board will provide strategic direction, approve national programmes and priorities, oversee partnerships and resources, and ensure that the chapter operates in accordance with GRAITE's mission, global policies, and applicable national laws.

Country Chapter Governance Board Chief

Each Country Chapter Governance Board will be headed by a Country Chapter Governance Board Chief, who will serve as the principal national governance leader and institutional representative of GRAITE within that country.

The Board Chief will provide leadership to the Country Chapter Governance Board, coordinate national strategy, facilitate engagement with the global organisation, and ensure alignment between national initiatives and GRAITE's international objectives.

National Research & Programme Leadership

Coordinates the implementation of research programmes, education initiatives, fellowships, grants, institutional collaborations, innovation projects, and other approved activities within the country. This function will support effective programme delivery and collaboration across national research communities.

National Research Fellows, Members & Collaborators

Researchers, scientists, engineers, educators, fellows, consultants, and institutional collaborators contribute their knowledge and expertise to the chapter's research, education, technology development, evaluation, and public-interest initiatives.

National Advisory & Expert Committees

Country Chapters may establish specialised advisory or expert committees in areas such as AI research, scientific development, AI safety, ethics, governance, education, industry collaboration, and public policy, according to their national priorities and institutional needs.

Global–National Governance Alignment

Local Autonomy. Global Standards. Shared Accountability.

GRAITE's international structure will operate through a coordinated relationship between its global governing institution and its Country Chapters. This model will enable national leadership and local initiative while preserving institutional consistency, scientific independence, and accountability across the international network.

Global Institutional Direction

The global governing institution will establish GRAITE's mission, strategic priorities, institutional identity, international research standards, core policies, and overarching governance principles. It will facilitate international collaboration, cross-country research programmes, knowledge exchange, and the development of shared institutional resources.

National Governance & Implementation

Each Country Chapter Governance Board will provide national oversight and guide the chapter's activities within the authority delegated to it. Chapters will develop country-specific programmes, establish local partnerships, mobilise research communities, and implement initiatives in accordance with applicable laws and GRAITE's global framework.

International Collaboration Across Chapters

Country Chapters will be encouraged to collaborate directly with one another through joint research programmes, shared laboratories, international fellowships, scientific exchanges, collaborative publications, technology development, and cross-border innovation initiatives.

Researchers and institutions may participate in collaborative programmes across multiple countries, enabling GRAITE to connect scientific expertise, infrastructure, and knowledge beyond national boundaries.

Common Institutional Standards

All Country Chapters will be expected to uphold GRAITE's common principles relating to scientific integrity, research quality, AI safety, ethical conduct, financial transparency, responsible partnerships, data protection, and institutional accountability.

National implementation may adapt to local laws and requirements, but such adaptations should not compromise GRAITE's fundamental mission, ethical commitments, or research standards.

Accountability & Institutional Integrity

Ensuring Consistency Across the Global Network

To maintain trust, credibility, and institutional integrity across countries, GRAITE will seek to establish common accountability mechanisms for its Country Chapters.

Governance & Policy Compliance

Country Chapters will operate under approved governance policies, delegated responsibilities, reporting requirements, and applicable national legal frameworks.

Scientific & Research Integrity

Research activities will be expected to follow appropriate scientific standards, ethical review procedures, methodological transparency, and responsible research practices.

Financial Transparency & Resource Management

Chapters will maintain appropriate financial controls, records, reporting mechanisms, and oversight of grants, donations, partnerships, and programme expenditure, consistent with applicable laws and GRAITE's financial governance policies.

Performance & Impact Reporting

Each Country Chapter will periodically report its research activities, programmes, partnerships, educational initiatives, achievements, challenges, and measurable contributions to GRAITE's mission.

Review & Institutional Development

The global organisation may establish periodic chapter reviews and evaluation mechanisms to assess governance effectiveness, research quality, institutional performance, and alignment with GRAITE's global objectives.

International Research Collaboration

Connecting Knowledge, Talent & Innovation Across Borders

One of GRAITE's central objectives is to enable scientific collaboration that transcends geographical and institutional boundaries. Its Country Chapters will provide the foundation for a distributed global research ecosystem in which knowledge, talent, resources, and opportunities can be shared internationally.

Through the global chapter network, GRAITE will seek to facilitate:

Joint International Research Projects
Cross-Border Research Fellowships & Scholarships
Global AI Safety & Governance Initiatives
Collaborative Research Laboratories
International Scientific Conferences & Symposia
Researcher & Academic Exchange Programmes
Shared Research Infrastructure & Knowledge Platforms
Industry–Academia–Government Partnerships
International Innovation Challenges
Collaborative Responses to Global Challenges

This network will help connect local scientific capabilities with global research opportunities, encouraging interdisciplinary thinking and collective progress on challenges that require international cooperation.

Establishing & Expanding Chapters

A Structured Approach to Global Expansion

GRAITE's international network will grow through the establishment of Country Chapters in countries where there is a strong opportunity to advance scientific research, responsible AI development, education, and international collaboration.

The establishment of a Country Chapter may involve:

Identification of National Research & Innovation Priorities
Appointment or Formation of a Founding Governance Board
Selection of the Country Chapter Governance Board Chief
Development of a National Institutional & Research Roadmap
Identification of Academic, Scientific & Industry Partners
Establishment of National Research & Programme Teams
Adoption of Governance, Ethics & Financial Policies
Formal Recognition or Registration, Where Required
Approval and Recognition Under GRAITE's Global Framework
Development of National Programmes & International Collaborations

Chapter formation, recognition, legal status, and operational authority will be subject to GRAITE's approved policies and the legal and regulatory requirements of the respective country.

Our Global Vision

One Global Research Community. A Shared Future for Humanity.

GRAITE aspires to build an international network of Country Chapters that connects scientific communities, institutions, and innovators in a shared commitment to advancing artificial intelligence responsibly.

By combining global research direction with strong national leadership, GRAITE will seek to create an institutional ecosystem in which local knowledge contributes to global progress, international collaboration strengthens national capabilities, and scientific discovery translates into meaningful benefits for society.

Every Country Chapter will be part of a larger global mission: to advance scientific excellence, foster responsible technological progress, strengthen international cooperation, and ensure that the benefits of increasingly advanced AI contribute to the long-term well-being of humanity.

One Global Mission. Many Nations. Boundless Collaboration.

Advancing Global AI Research Through Local Leadership and International Cooperation.
