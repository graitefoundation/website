Chapters & Governance

A global institutional framework connecting scientific independence, responsible governance, national leadership, and international collaboration.

GRAITE's governance framework is designed to support scientific independence, strategic accountability, ethical conduct, financial transparency, and responsible AI research.

Institutional Governance

A Framework for Trust, Independence & Accountability

As a global research organisation, GRAITE will operate through an integrated institutional structure that combines international leadership with national representation and local participation. This framework will balance global strategic direction with delegated country-level responsibilities, ensuring consistency in institutional values while allowing each country to address its own research priorities, opportunities, and societal needs.

Clearly defined governance responsibilities, independent oversight, common institutional standards, and transparent accountability will form the foundation of GRAITE's long-term institutional development.

Global Governance Structure

International Leadership & Institutional Direction

Board of Trustees / Global Governing Board

Provides the highest level of institutional governance and strategic oversight within GRAITE. It safeguards the organisation's mission, values, independence, and long-term institutional integrity. Its responsibilities will include approving major policies and budgets, overseeing institutional risks and compliance, and ensuring accountability across the global organisation.

Global Advisory Council

Brings together recognised experts and distinguished professionals from AI research, science, engineering, AI safety, ethics, governance, education, public policy, and social innovation. It provides strategic and scientific advice on global research priorities, emerging technologies, international collaboration, scientific standards, and the opportunities and risks associated with increasingly advanced AI.

Executive Director

Leads the execution of GRAITE's global strategy and oversees organisational management, international partnerships, fundraising, programme delivery, and operational performance. The Executive Director translates institutional priorities into programmes, coordinates global activities, and supports collaboration across the international network.

Global Research & Programme Leadership

Coordinates international research programmes, fellowships, scholarships, education initiatives, grant-funded activities, innovation projects, commercial engagements, and knowledge platforms. This function facilitates collaboration across research domains and supports the effective implementation of GRAITE's global programmes.

Researchers, Fellows & Global Collaborators

Researchers, scientists, engineers, educators, fellows, consultants, and institutional partners contribute scientific expertise, technical development, education, evaluation, advisory services, and collaborative research. Together, they form the wider knowledge and innovation community through which GRAITE advances its mission.

Independent Oversight & Accountability

Protecting Scientific Integrity & Public Trust

GRAITE will seek to establish independent oversight and accountability mechanisms proportionate to its activities, scale, and legal structure. These mechanisms will help ensure that scientific quality, ethical conduct, financial responsibility, and institutional performance remain central to its operations.

Scientific Review & Research Integrity

Reviews research quality, scientific merit, methodological integrity, reproducibility, and the validity of research outcomes. It will promote independent evaluation, responsible publication, transparent research practices, and appropriate management of conflicts of interest.

AI Safety, Ethics & Responsible Technology

Advises on AI-related risks, ethical considerations, responsible development practices, appropriate safeguards, human oversight, and deployment considerations. It will help ensure that safety and ethical principles are considered throughout the AI lifecycle.

Audit, Finance & Institutional Transparency

Oversees financial governance, internal controls, grant utilisation, financial risk management, and independent audits where applicable. It will strengthen transparency, responsible resource management, and institutional accountability.

Impact Evaluation & Stakeholder Engagement

Assesses research outcomes, educational reach, institutional development, societal benefits, and progress against GRAITE's mission. Appropriate channels will also be developed for researchers, communities, policymakers, and affected stakeholders to contribute perspectives on research, innovation, and public-interest priorities.

The precise composition, authority, reporting relationships, and legal status of governance and oversight bodies will be established through GRAITE's governing documents and applicable legal requirements.

Global Chapters Network

One Global Mission. Multiple Country Chapters. Shared Responsibility.

GRAITE envisions a globally connected network of Country Chapters bringing together researchers, scientists, engineers, academic institutions, technology organisations, policymakers, and social innovators across nations.

Each Country Chapter will serve as a national platform for advancing AI research, scientific collaboration, education, responsible technology development, and public-interest innovation. Together, these chapters will form an interconnected international research community working towards GRAITE's shared mission of advancing safe, responsible, and transformative AI for humanity.

Country Chapter Governance

National Leadership Within a Unified Global Framework

Each Country Chapter will have its own governance structure to provide national leadership, strategic direction, operational oversight, and accountability. This structure will reflect local institutional requirements while remaining consistent with GRAITE's global governance principles, policies, and standards.

Country Chapter Governance Board

Each Country Chapter will be governed by a Country Chapter Governance Board responsible for guiding and overseeing the chapter's national activities. The Board will establish national priorities, approve programmes within its delegated authority, oversee partnerships and resources, and ensure compliance with GRAITE's global framework and applicable national laws.

Country Chapter Governance Board Chief

Each Country Chapter Governance Board will be headed by a Country Chapter Governance Board Chief, who will serve as the principal national governance leader and institutional representative of GRAITE within that country.

The Board Chief will lead the national governance process, coordinate the work of the Country Chapter Governance Board, guide the chapter's strategic direction, and facilitate engagement with GRAITE's global leadership and other Country Chapters.

National Research & Programme Leadership

Coordinates the implementation of national research programmes, education initiatives, fellowships, grants, institutional collaborations, innovation projects, and other approved activities. This function supports programme delivery and collaboration across national research communities.

National Researchers, Fellows, Members & Collaborators

Researchers, scientists, engineers, educators, fellows, consultants, and institutional collaborators contribute their knowledge and expertise to the chapter's research, education, technology development, evaluation, and public-interest initiatives.

National Advisory & Expert Committees

Country Chapters may establish specialised advisory or expert committees in areas such as AI research, scientific development, AI safety, ethics, governance, education, industry collaboration, and public policy, according to their national priorities and institutional needs.

Global–National Coordination

Local Autonomy. Global Standards. Shared Accountability.

GRAITE's international structure will operate through a coordinated relationship between its global governing institution and its Country Chapters. This model will combine a unified global mission and common institutional standards with delegated national authority and locally responsive implementation.

Global Direction & Institutional Standards

The global governing institution will establish GRAITE's mission, strategic priorities, institutional identity, core policies, international research standards, and overarching governance principles. It will coordinate international programmes, facilitate cross-country collaboration, and maintain the consistency and integrity of the global network.

National Authority & Implementation

Each Country Chapter Governance Board will guide national activities within its delegated authority. Chapters will develop country-specific programmes, establish local partnerships, mobilise research communities, and implement initiatives in accordance with applicable laws and GRAITE's global framework.

International Collaboration & Knowledge Exchange

Country Chapters will be encouraged to collaborate through joint research projects, shared laboratories, international fellowships, scientific exchanges, collaborative publications, technology development, and cross-border innovation initiatives.

Researchers and institutions may participate in programmes across multiple countries, enabling GRAITE to connect scientific expertise, infrastructure, talent, and knowledge beyond national boundaries.

All chapters will uphold common principles relating to scientific integrity, research quality, AI safety, ethical conduct, financial transparency, responsible partnerships, data protection, and institutional accountability. National implementation may adapt to local requirements without compromising GRAITE's fundamental mission and commitments.

Chapter Accountability & Development

Institutional Recognition, Compliance & Global Expansion

GRAITE will seek to establish a common framework for recognising, developing, monitoring, and strengthening its Country Chapters. This will support institutional consistency while allowing chapters to evolve according to their national research ecosystems and operational requirements.

Governance, Legal & Policy Compliance

Country Chapters will operate under approved governance policies, clearly delegated responsibilities, reporting requirements, and applicable national legal frameworks. Their legal status, authority, and relationship with the global organisation will be formally defined.

Financial Management & Transparency

Chapters will maintain appropriate financial controls, records, and reporting mechanisms covering grants, donations, partnerships, programme expenditure, and other resources, consistent with applicable laws and GRAITE's financial governance policies.

Research Quality & Institutional Performance

Research activities will follow appropriate scientific standards, ethical review procedures, methodological transparency, and responsible research practices. Chapters will periodically report their programmes, partnerships, achievements, challenges, and measurable contributions to GRAITE's mission.

Chapter Establishment & Recognition

The establishment of a Country Chapter may involve:

Identification of National Research & Innovation Priorities
Formation of a Founding Country Chapter Governance Board
Appointment of the Country Chapter Governance Board Chief
Development of a National Institutional & Research Roadmap
Identification of Academic, Scientific & Industry Partners
Formation of National Research & Programme Teams
Adoption of Governance, Ethics & Financial Policies
Formal Registration or Legal Recognition, Where Required
Approval and Recognition Under GRAITE's Global Framework
Development of National Programmes & International Collaborations

Chapter formation, recognition, legal status, and operational authority will be subject to GRAITE's approved policies and the legal and regulatory requirements of the respective country. The global organisation may establish periodic reviews to assess governance effectiveness, research quality, institutional performance, and alignment with its global objectives.

Long-Term Institutional Purpose

Building a Trusted Global Institution for AI & Humanity

GRAITE aspires to become a trusted global platform connecting scientific research, advanced AI development, responsible technology governance, education, and public-interest innovation.

Through international collaboration, independent research, talent development, and sustainable engagement models, GRAITE will work to ensure that advances in artificial intelligence are not measured solely by computational power or commercial value, but also by their safety, reliability, accessibility, and contribution to human well-being.

By combining global research direction with strong national leadership, GRAITE will seek to create an institutional ecosystem in which local knowledge contributes to global progress, international collaboration strengthens national capabilities, and scientific discovery translates into meaningful benefits for society.

One Global Mission. Many Nations. A Shared Future.

GRAITE's enduring purpose is to advance global research in AI technologies while fostering the responsible development, deployment, and stewardship of increasingly advanced AI for the long-term benefit of humanity.
