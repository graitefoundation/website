Membership Policy & Terms

A Voluntary Global Community for Knowledge, Research & Innovation

GRAITE (Global Research in AI Technologies and Engineering) welcomes individuals who wish to associate with its mission of advancing responsible Artificial Intelligence through research, education, engineering, knowledge sharing and global collaboration.

Membership of GRAITE is voluntary and free of charge. It is intended to provide a platform for intellectual exchange, voluntary contributions and participation in activities aligned with GRAITE's mission. Membership does not create any employment, commercial, financial or other contractual relationship beyond the terms expressly accepted under this policy.

01 / NATURE OF MEMBERSHIP

Voluntary Association

GRAITE membership represents an individual's voluntary association with a global knowledge and research community. It is intended primarily to encourage intellectual participation, professional networking, knowledge exchange and voluntary contributions towards GRAITE's research, education and engineering missions.

Membership is not a paid service, commercial arrangement, professional appointment or guarantee of any particular opportunity, recognition, position or benefit.

Membership is free of charge. GRAITE does not require members to provide any financial contribution, professional service, research output or other deliverable as a condition of ordinary membership.

02 / EMPLOYMENT & PROFESSIONAL STATUS

No Employment or Contractual Engagement

Membership of GRAITE shall not be construed as creating or implying:

  • An employer–employee relationship.
  • A consultancy, retainership or professional services arrangement.
  • A partnership, joint venture, agency or representative relationship.
  • A fiduciary relationship or entitlement to institutional remuneration.
  • Any right to salary, wages, honorarium, commission, benefits, incentives or compensation.
  • Any right to employment, appointment, promotion or future engagement with GRAITE.

Members remain independent individuals and are responsible for their own employment, professional activities, taxes, statutory obligations and personal commitments.

Any employment, consultancy, research contract, paid assignment or formal institutional appointment must be established separately through an expressly authorised written agreement.

03 / FINANCIAL RIGHTS & ENTITLEMENTS

No Financial Entitlement or Obligation

Membership does not create any financial liability or payment obligation on GRAITE towards a member.

Members shall not be entitled to claim:

  • Salary, remuneration, honorarium or professional fees.
  • Travel, accommodation, communication or other expenses.
  • Reimbursement for voluntary work, research, advice or contributions.
  • Funding, grants, scholarships, fellowships or research support solely by virtue of membership.
  • Compensation for time, effort, resources or opportunities voluntarily contributed.
  • Any share in GRAITE's income, assets, intellectual property or commercial activities.

Any financial support, grant, reimbursement or paid engagement specifically approved by GRAITE shall be governed by a separate written arrangement and its applicable conditions.

04 / VOLUNTARY CONTRIBUTIONS

Knowledge Sharing on Free Will

Members may voluntarily contribute knowledge, ideas, research inputs, discussions, technical expertise, educational material, recommendations and other intellectual contributions towards GRAITE's objectives.

Such participation is entirely voluntary. Unless separately agreed in writing, GRAITE is not obligated to assign work, provide resources, accept a contribution, implement a recommendation or compensate a member for any contribution.

Members are not obligated to maintain any minimum level of participation, attendance, research activity or contribution. They may participate according to their own availability and interests, subject to applicable policies.

Members remain responsible for ensuring that their contributions are lawful and do not knowingly violate the rights of third parties, confidentiality obligations or applicable intellectual property rights.

05 / REPRESENTATION & INSTITUTIONAL AUTHORITY

Membership Does Not Confer Authority

Membership alone does not authorise any individual to represent, speak for, act on behalf of, or create any obligation for GRAITE or any of its Country Chapters.

Unless expressly authorised in writing by the competent GRAITE authority, members shall not:

  • Represent themselves as an official spokesperson, authorised representative or institutional decision-maker of GRAITE.
  • Make commitments, promises, assurances or declarations on behalf of GRAITE.
  • Enter into agreements, contracts, financial arrangements or partnerships in GRAITE's name.
  • Solicit funds, donations, sponsorships or commercial commitments using GRAITE's name or identity.
  • Issue official statements, certificates, appointments, endorsements or institutional communications.
  • Use GRAITE's name, logo, identity or affiliation to imply an authority or endorsement that has not been granted.
  • Claim that their personal opinions, research positions or activities constitute official GRAITE positions.

Any formal designation, leadership role, committee responsibility, council appointment or authority to represent GRAITE must be separately approved and documented by the competent institutional authority.

06 / MEMBERSHIP CATEGORIES

Members & Council Participation

GRAITE may provide different categories of membership and institutional participation, including:

Member: An individual associated with GRAITE for knowledge exchange, networking, voluntary participation and contribution towards its mission.

Council: An individual considered for participation in a recognised council, advisory body or governance-related structure. Council participation is subject to separate eligibility assessment, approval and formal appointment by the competent GRAITE authority.

Selecting the Council category in a membership application does not automatically confer council membership, governance authority, an official designation or any institutional position.

The responsibilities, tenure, scope of authority and conditions of any formally constituted council or committee shall be defined separately.

07 / INDEPENDENCE & PERSONAL RESPONSIBILITY

Independent Professional and Personal Activities

Members may continue their independent employment, business, academic, research and professional activities. Membership does not restrict an individual's independent professional identity or affiliations.

Members are solely responsible for their own statements, opinions, publications, research, recommendations, projects and external engagements.

Personal views expressed by members shall not be attributed to GRAITE unless expressly adopted or authorised as an official institutional position.

GRAITE does not assume responsibility for independent activities undertaken by members outside an expressly authorised institutional assignment or written agreement.

08 / INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & CONTRIBUTIONS

Ownership and Use of Contributions

Members retain ownership of intellectual property that they independently create or own, subject to applicable law and third-party rights.

Submission of a research paper, article, idea, document, software, design or other material does not automatically transfer its ownership to GRAITE.

Where a member voluntarily submits material for GRAITE's consideration, publication, distribution or use, the scope of permitted use, attribution, licensing and ownership shall be determined by the applicable submission terms or a separate written agreement.

Members must not submit confidential, proprietary or third-party material without the necessary rights and permissions.

09 / CONFIDENTIALITY & MEMBER CONDUCT

Responsible Participation

Members are expected to maintain professional conduct, respect differing viewpoints and support a collaborative, inclusive and responsible knowledge-sharing environment.

Members shall not misuse access to GRAITE's information, networks, events, platforms, institutional resources or other members' personal information.

Information expressly identified as confidential or shared under a confidentiality obligation must not be disclosed or misused. Membership itself does not grant access to confidential, restricted or proprietary institutional information.

Members must comply with applicable laws, GRAITE policies, research integrity requirements, event guidelines and relevant codes of conduct.

Scope of Institutional Responsibility

GRAITE endeavours to provide a constructive platform for research, education, collaboration and knowledge exchange. However, membership is voluntary and participation is undertaken at the member's own discretion and responsibility.

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, GRAITE, its governing bodies, officers, employees, authorised representatives and affiliated chapters shall not be responsible for indirect, incidental, special or consequential losses, loss of income, business opportunities, anticipated benefits or reputational expectations arising from voluntary membership or participation.

GRAITE shall not be responsible for:

  • A member's personal decisions, independent activities, statements or professional commitments.
  • Opinions, advice, research findings or recommendations shared by other members.
  • The accuracy, completeness or suitability of information exchanged through voluntary discussions or knowledge platforms.
  • The non-availability, discontinuation or modification of events, programmes, opportunities or community activities.
  • Any unauthorised representation, promise, commitment or transaction undertaken by a member in GRAITE's name.
  • Any expectation of funding, employment, recognition, appointment, access or professional advancement arising solely from membership.

Nothing in this policy shall exclude or limit liability to the extent such exclusion or limitation is prohibited by applicable law, including liability arising from fraud, wilful misconduct or other non-excludable legal obligations.

Membership does not create any guarantee, warranty or assurance of research outcomes, professional opportunities, institutional support or specific benefits.

11 / MEMBER RESPONSIBILITY & INDEMNITY

Unauthorised Acts and Third-Party Claims

To the extent permitted by applicable law, members shall be responsible for their own unlawful acts, misrepresentations, unauthorised commitments and violations of third-party rights.

A member may be required to indemnify GRAITE against reasonable, documented losses, liabilities, costs and expenses arising directly from the member's fraud, wilful misconduct, unlawful conduct, material breach of this policy or unauthorised use of GRAITE's identity, subject to applicable law.

This provision does not make members responsible for GRAITE's independent acts or obligations, nor does it create an obligation beyond what is permitted by law.

12 / MEMBERSHIP STATUS & TERMINATION

Voluntary Continuation and Withdrawal

Membership is voluntary and may be discontinued by a member at any time by submitting a request to GRAITE through its designated communication channel.

GRAITE reserves the right to decline, suspend or terminate membership where there is a material breach of this policy, misuse of institutional identity, misconduct, unlawful activity or conduct inconsistent with GRAITE's mission and values.

GRAITE may also review membership records and status as part of its institutional governance and chapter administration.

Termination or withdrawal of membership does not create any entitlement to compensation, reimbursement or other financial claim, except where a separate written agreement or applicable law provides otherwise.

13 / PRIVACY & MEMBER INFORMATION

Use of Membership Information

Information submitted during the membership process, including name, country, LinkedIn profile and areas of interest, may be used for membership review, chapter coordination, institutional communication and administration.

GRAITE shall handle personal information in accordance with its applicable privacy policy and relevant data protection laws. Personal information shall not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis.

Members should not submit sensitive personal information or confidential third-party information through the membership form.

14 / POLICY ADMINISTRATION

Interpretation & Amendments

GRAITE may amend, update or replace this Membership Policy from time to time in accordance with its institutional governance procedures.

Updated policies shall be published through GRAITE's official communication channels. Members may be required to review and accept material changes where appropriate.

If any provision of this policy is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue to apply to the extent permitted by applicable law.
