Engineering a Safe, Responsible, and Transformative AI for humanity

We believe that the future of artificial intelligence must be shaped not only by technological capability, but also by scientific integrity, human responsibility, safety, and a commitment to the common good

About GRAITE

We are a global, multidisciplinary network of scientists, researchers, technologists, engineers, social innovators, educators, and policy experts working collaboratively across disciplines, sectors, and borders to address humanity's most pressing challenges through AI research, technological innovation, education, and responsible deployment.

VISION & MISSION

To research, discover, develop AI-driven solutions that improve human well-being, strengthen institutions, and accelerate inclusive and sustainable societal progress.

Foster international collaboration to anticipate emerging opportunities and risks, reduce potential harms, and advance AI capabilities in ways that serve the long-term interests of humanity.

OUR STRATEGIC PILLARS

Advanced AI for Humanity

Support research and international cooperation to ensure that increasingly capable AI systems remain beneficial, controllable, and aligned with human goals, fundamental rights, and societal values.

AI for Global Challenges

Encourage practical AI applications across healthcare, education, climate, agriculture, industry, public services, and economic development, critical to human well-being.

AI Ethics, Governance & Policy

Bring together researchers, industry leaders, civil society, and policymakers to develop evidence-informed governance frameworks, standards to AI risks and opportunities.

Global Collaboration & Knowledge Exchange

Build a trusted international network that connects disciplines, institutions, and countries to share research, develop solutions, establish best practices, and translate AI innovation into measurable societal benefits.

AI Research & Scientific Discovery

Advance fundamental and applied AI research, interdisciplinary collaboration, and knowledge sharing to expand scientific understanding and unlock new possibilities.

Education & Capacity Building

Strengthen AI literacy, research capabilities, technical skills, and public understanding. Empower individuals and institutions to participate in development and adoption of AI.

Inclusive & Equitable Innovation

Promote broad access to AI knowledge, research opportunities, and technological benefits while addressing bias, inequality, privacy, discrimination, and the risk of leaving communities behind.
