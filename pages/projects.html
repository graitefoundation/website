AI Projects & Initiatives

Exploring the possibilities of Artificial Intelligence through scientific research, education, capacity building and practical engineering solutions. GRAITE seeks to encourage projects that advance responsible AI and create measurable value for society, science and industry.

GRAITE envisions a global portfolio of collaborative AI initiatives. Projects may be developed by research teams, universities, country chapters, industry partners, educators and independent innovators.
